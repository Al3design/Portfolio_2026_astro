I am Alejandro Delgado, a multimedia designer based in Buenos Aires, Argentina. I work across UX/UI, web interfaces, graphic design and 3D, combining visual design with implementation.

My projects range from interface design in Figma and Adobe XD to web development with HTML, CSS, Sass, Astro and React. I also work with Photoshop and Illustrator, and explore modeling, lighting, animation and compositing with tools such as 3ds Max, V-Ray and After Effects.

GitCron is my personal project for working with Git through a visual interface, bringing together my workflow, OpenSpec and AI assistance. In client work, projects such as OdontoPau connect web design with visual identity, graphic materials and personalized 3D-printed pieces.

Alongside that work, I administer the Moodle platform in TED at INTI. My earlier roles at the institute include project coordination and IT responsibilities, marketing, and institutional web interfaces. This portfolio brings those experiences together with personal, community and academic projects, identifying my contribution and the resources provided by others.
