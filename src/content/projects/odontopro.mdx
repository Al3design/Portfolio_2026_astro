        title: A system to support dental care
        text: >-
          I started OdontoPro in April 2026 for a private client, with the aim
          of bringing patient records, clinical history, prescriptions, billing
          and appointment-related communication into a dental practice
          management application.


          The project is still in early development. My current work focuses on
          testing its multi-clinic data foundation: information belonging to
          different clinics must remain separate, and changes must preserve the
          records that need to be kept. These are areas under investigation and
          testing, rather than established production outcomes.
      - _template: section
        title: 'The current challenge: separating clinic data'
        text: >-
          The focus is on checking that one clinic’s information does not mix
          with another’s, and that operations do not overwrite or delete records
          unintentionally. The challenge involves both isolating data and
          preserving it as the application evolves.


          This work is still being tested. Multi-clinic reliability is a
          development objective, not a completed result. Google Calendar
          synchronization and WhatsApp appointment messages remain planned
          future integrations.
      - _template: section
        title: From scheduling to continuity of care
        text: >-
          The intended scope follows the relationship between the practice and
          its patients: scheduling appointments, consulting previous records,
          documenting care and continuing with administrative tasks.


          Prescriptions, billing and appointment communication are part of that
          intended scope. This article does not present the full workflow as
          complete: the current priority is the multi-clinic data foundation.
          The selected images show a directory and a test patient record.
